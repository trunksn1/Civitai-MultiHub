What can I add?
Creator
Name, such as Jenescript, or the creator-page URL.
Model / LoRA
Page URL, such as civitai.com/models/2563220, or just 2563220. This number identifies the whole model.
Specific version
A model URL containing modelVersionId=… preselects that exact version. Without it, MultiHub lets you choose the versions.
Collection
The URL of a public Civitai image collection.
